## Constraints And Review Focus

250-350 nodes, levels 1-3; six guide variations; two plans, three quizzes each.
No dependencies, migrations, quota changes, real-engine/network tests or live
DB verification writes. Existing London/Vienna/Grand Prix and Black Caro stay.

1. White e4 c6 must not be confused with Black's Caro-Kann training.
2. Different first replies within one repertoire must both match correctly.
3. Shared positions preserve history and do not duplicate accepted e4.
4. Plans/exercises must be unique across existing and new content.
5. Claims about pawns and bishop development must match the actual board.
